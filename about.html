About Me

I'm an AI Engineer in Berlin with around three years of building production LLM systems in a regulated domain (insurance and financial advice), and 5+ years of applied ML overall across RAG, AI agents, NLP, document AI and Python/FastAPI services on Kubernetes.

Since October 2023 I've been a Machine Learning Engineer at Muffintech GmbH, a start-up building conversational AI for the insurance and financial-advisory industry in Germany and Austria. I work on AI systems that generate and validate software workflows, retrieval with verifiable citations, a realtime voice agent and the multi-tenant platform underneath.

Before that I was an R&D Engineer at STTech GmbH in Munich (document AI, NER/NEL, LLM fine-tuning), and before that a research assistant at the Institute of Computational Neuroscience (UKE) in Hamburg. There I co-authored the first-place solution for both ISIC 2019 skin lesion classification tasks. I hold an M.Sc. in Mechatronics from TU Hamburg, specialising in Intelligent Systems and Robotics.
